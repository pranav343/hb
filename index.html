Why Hii Blend

Fresh isn't a flavour. It's a deadline.

Every bottle moves from press to your hands within 24 hours — or it doesn't leave our kitchen.

🥬

Whole produce only

No concentrates, no syrups, no "natural-identical" anything. If it isn't a fruit, vegetable, nut or spice, it isn't in the bottle.

❄️

Cold-pressed, cold-kept

Slow hydraulic pressing keeps enzymes and nutrients intact, and the cold chain holds until the moment you open it.

🧾

Full transparency

Ingredients, calories and price on every item. What's on the label is exactly what's in the glass.

About us

Made by people who read ingredient labels

Hii Blend started with a simple frustration: "healthy" drinks that were mostly sugar, water and marketing. So we built a menu the way we'd want it as customers — real produce, honest portions, and every number printed where you can see it.

Today we press, blend and bottle daily, in small batches, from our outlet at Second Floor, Building 8, Khan Market, New Delhi — with indoor seating for dine-in and home delivery across nearby areas. From a 60 ml immunity shot to a supermatcha or a full-meal wrap, everything is made fresh on order.
